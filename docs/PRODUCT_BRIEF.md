# Product Brief

## Product

**BLOOM**<br>
**Tagline:** Your body, your pace.

BLOOM is a mobile weight-management companion for women. The first launch is designed around postpartum recovery, including people who want to lose weight gently, gain weight healthily, or maintain and feel steady.

## Product promise

BLOOM helps users understand trends, plan culturally relevant meals, and care for themselves without shame, punishment, or alarm over normal body changes.

## Launch audience and regions

- Adults aged 18 and older
- Postpartum users as the primary launch audience
- Nigeria and the United States
- English language
- Metric and imperial display preferences

The MVP is not designed for pregnancy weight management or minors. A user who reports a current pregnancy receives a gentle boundary and is directed to qualified care rather than a weight-target plan.

## Core differentiators

1. Healthy weight gain is a first-class goal alongside loss and maintenance.
2. No shame mechanics, punitive streaks, red calorie warnings, or transformation language.
3. Optional cycle and postpartum context explains normal fluctuations.
4. Curated Nigerian and US meal suggestions account for goals, preferences, and allergen exclusions.
5. Progress emphasizes weekly trends and steady care rather than daily spikes.

## Core product areas

- Seven-step onboarding and personalized plan reveal
- Today screen with meals, context, trend, and quick logging
- Weekly meal planning and swaps
- Weight trend and optional private progress photos
- Optional cycle and postpartum insights
- Separate community spaces for gaining/recovery and gentle loss
- Free and Pro access managed through RevenueCat
- Safety check-ins and verified regional support resources
- Settings, privacy, notifications, account export, and deletion

## Free and Pro access

Free users receive onboarding, three meal suggestions per week, basic weight logging, and read-only community access.

Pro users receive full weekly meal plans and swaps, cycle-aware insights, progress trends, community posting and support reactions, and personalized plan adjustments.

The planned price is USD 9.99 monthly or USD 59.99 annually, with storefront-localized display and final store configuration reviewed before release.

## Explicit non-goals for MVP

- Medical diagnosis or treatment
- Pregnancy weight-target planning
- Support for minors
- Exact GPS collection
- Automatic food recognition
- Calorie punishment, weigh-in punishment, public weight comparisons, or before/after mechanics
- Direct messaging between community members
- Custom payment receipt validation
- Languages other than English
- Public web consumer application

## Success criteria

- Users can complete onboarding for loss, gain, or maintenance without encountering shame-based copy.
- Users receive safe, explainable, region-appropriate plans with allergen exclusions enforced.
- Daily fluctuations never dominate the progress experience.
- Community access is separated by emotional context and protected by reporting, blocking, moderation, and audit controls.
- All production safety, clinical, legal, security, accessibility, and store gates pass.
