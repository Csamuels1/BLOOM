# Brand and Copy Guide

## Personality

Warm, grounded, patient, non-judgmental, and quietly confident. BLOOM should feel like a knowledgeable friend, not a drill sergeant or clinical chart.

## Visual tokens

| Token | Value | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary Terracotta | `#C97C5D` | Primary actions and warmth |
| Secondary Sage | `#8A9A5B` | Growth and positive states |
| Background Ivory | `#FDF8F3` | Default app background |
| Text Plum | `#3B2A35` | Primary text |
| Accent Gold | `#D4A373` | Highlights and gentle celebrations |
| Blush | `#E8B4B8` | Soft emphasis |
| Muted Sage | `#C8D0B8` | Cards and subtle surfaces |
| Caution Amber | `#D98E4A` | Genuine safety notices only |

Pure white is not a default background. Red is not used for calories, weight, missed logs, or ordinary errors.

## Typography and shape

- Display: Fraunces semibold, 32px
- Page title: Fraunces semibold, 24px
- Section title: Manrope semibold, 18px
- Body: Manrope regular, 15px
- Caption: Manrope medium, 13px
- Cards: 16px radius
- Buttons: 12px radius
- Pills: full radius
- Elevation: soft and minimal

## Interaction personality

Interaction density should be high while emotional pressure remains low. Use responsive cards, bottom sheets, contextual transitions, chart exploration, swipe or tap-based meal swaps, immediate validation, haptics for meaningful confirmation, skeleton loading, and optimistic updates where rollback is safe.

Avoid aggressive gamification, flashing feedback, loud confetti, anxiety-inducing countdowns, manipulative urgency, or endless engagement loops. Respect reduced-motion settings.

## Voice rules

Use language that normalizes body changes and offers choices.

Preferred examples:

- "Weight shifts for many reasons. This isn't a setback."
- "You're building a steady rhythm."
- "This helps us personalize your plan. Nothing here is used to judge you."
- "If things feel hard right now, here are people who can help."

Prohibited language includes:

- "You failed"
- "You went over"
- "Stay strong"
- "Crush your goals"
- "Dream body"
- "Before and after"
- Moral labels such as "good food," "bad food," "clean," or "cheat meal"

## Copy review test

For every user-facing sentence, ask: "Could this make someone feel worse about their body on a hard day?" If yes, rewrite it.

Voice-sensitive strings must be centralized, reviewed, and checked by automated tests for prohibited phrases.
